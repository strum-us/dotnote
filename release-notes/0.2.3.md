# Dotnote 0.2.3 — Translate languages and DALL·E

## AI

- **Translate to** opens a language submenu in the AI bar — pick a target and run in one step
- **Settings › AI › Translation** sets your default target (from your browser locale until you change it)

## AI images

- **OpenAI DALL·E 2 and DALL·E 3** join Gemini in **Settings › AI Image** — use the same OpenAI key as text models
