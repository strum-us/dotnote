# Dotnote 0.2.7 — Nano Banana and sharper image prompts

## AI images

- **Nano Banana first** — The default Gemini image model shows as **Nano Banana · Gemini 2.5 Flash Image** in the AI bar and Settings so it matches what people search for
- **Prompts tuned for images** — Image generation sends a short visual brief from your selection or nearby text instead of the whole note, and respects OpenAI DALL·E prompt length limits so DALL·E 3 and DALL·E 2 fail less often on long notes
