# License FAQ (plain-language summary)

Dotnote is **source-available**, not open source. The source code in this repository is licensed under the [Sustainable Use License 1.0](../LICENSE.md) (SUL).

This page is a convenience summary. It is not legal advice, and if it differs from [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md), LICENSE.md controls.

## The short version

- Use Dotnote for yourself, or inside your own company, for free.
- Modify it for those purposes, for free.
- Share it with others only for free and for non-commercial purposes, with the license and copyright notices intact and a note saying you modified it.
- Anything commercial involving other people — hosting it for them, selling it, white-labeling it, bundling it into a product — needs a separate commercial license. Contact support@dotnote.cloud.

## What is allowed

| Use | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Install Dotnote into your own Cloudflare account and use it personally | Yes |
| Use Dotnote inside your company for your team's own notes | Yes |
| Modify the code for your own personal or internal company use | Yes |
| Redistribute the code (modified or not) free of charge, for non-commercial purposes, with notices intact | Yes |
| Build a separate integration that talks to your own Dotnote deployment | Yes |
| Study the code, report bugs, suggest changes | Yes |

## What is not allowed without a commercial license

| Use | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Offer Dotnote (or a product built mainly on it) to customers for money | No |
| Run a hosted/managed Dotnote service for third parties, paid or as part of a paid bundle | No |

| Sell the installer, or charge third parties to deploy Dotnote for them as a service | No |
| Remove or hide license, copyright, or trademark notices | No |
| Use the Dotnote name or logo for your product or service | No — see [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md) |

## Common questions

**Can my company use Dotnote internally without paying?**
Yes. Internal business use is expressly permitted, including on your own Cloudflare account.

**Can I fork Dotnote and give my fork away for free?**
You may distribute it free of charge for non-commercial purposes, keeping the license and notices, and marking your changes. You may not call it Dotnote or use the Dotnote logo. Offering that fork as a product or service to others — especially as a substitute for Dotnote — is outside the permitted purpose; ask us first if you are unsure.

**Can I charge clients to install Dotnote into their Cloudflare account?**
Providing the software to others is only permitted free of charge for non-commercial purposes. Paid deployment, resale, or managed operation for third parties requires a commercial license.

**Can I use Dotnote code inside my own commercial product?**
Only if Dotnote is not being provided to your customers as the product's functionality. If your customers get Dotnote (or a notes product built on it), you need a commercial license.

**Is Dotnote open source?**
No. The SUL is a source-available (fair-code) license, not an OSI-approved open source license. Please describe it as "source-available under the Sustainable Use License."

**What about the notes I write?**
They are yours. The license covers the software, not your content. Your notes stay in your Cloudflare R2 bucket as plain `.md` files.

**How do I get a commercial license?**
Email support@dotnote.cloud with what you plan to build or offer.
